The SVG files in this directory are Google Material Design icons.

Source: https://fonts.google.com/icons (Material Design Icons)
Upstream: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons
Licence: Apache License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)

They are bundled here as monochrome 24x24 glyphs and recoloured at runtime to
match the active light/dark theme (see src/utils/IconLoader.cpp).
